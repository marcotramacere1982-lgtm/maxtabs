    Sequence

    Tap a bar's dots to change the accent: weak, medium, strong. Drag the numbered handle to reorder the bars.

      Accents: tap any dot to cycle it from weak to medium to strong (for example, in a 7/8 bar you can mark a 2+2+3 by accenting the 1st, 3rd and 5th beat). Spacebar starts and pauses playback (it resumes where it left off), Backspace resets the loop to the first bar without starting it: F or the Live button toggles the full screen player (Esc to leave it), all of them work as long as you're not typing in a field. The sequence is saved in this browser.

      Drum Rudiments

      The 40 international Percussive Arts Society (PAS) rudiments, ready to load into the metronome with their accent pattern. "Load" replaces the current sequence with the rudiment; "Add to end" adds it as a new bar at the end.

      The metronome can only play weak, medium or strong accents on beats of equal length: bounced rolls, flams and drags are therefore simplified to an accent on the main note, without the "grace note" that precedes them. For the exact execution you still need a method book or a teacher: what you'll find here is the timing and accent pattern to practise on.
